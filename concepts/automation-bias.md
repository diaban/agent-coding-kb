# Automation bias

## Claim

A human who approves hundreds of correct proposals ends up approving the incorrect ones. Approval by a human is not evidence of attention.

## Background

Automation bias is well documented in piloting and in medical diagnosis: people over-rely on a system that is usually right and stop checking it.

## Consequence for the figures

Google's "generated by AI and approved by engineers" says nothing about the attention given to each approval. The same applies to any metric that counts human sign-offs.

## Objections

- No measurement of this effect on code review is cited in this base. It is an inference from other fields.
