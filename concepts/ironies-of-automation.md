# Ironies of automation

## Claim

The main risk is not that agents write bad code. It is that humans lose the ability to notice.

## The paradox

In 1983, Lisanne Bainbridge described a paradox observed in aviation and industry [S7]. The more a system is automated, the more the human merely supervises. They then lose the skill needed to take over, at the exact moment the automation fails on a hard case.

## Evidence in software

In a trial run by Anthropic in January 2026, the group coding with AI scored 50% on a comprehension quiz, against 67% for the group coding by hand (via [S3]).

## Objections

- One trial, reported through a secondary source. To verify against the original publication, including sample size and task type.
- A comprehension quiz right after a task does not measure long-term skill.
