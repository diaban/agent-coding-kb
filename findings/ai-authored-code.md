# AI is the main author of new code in several large companies

## Claim

Within roughly eighteen months, AI went from assisting to writing the majority of new code in several large companies. The trend is consistent wherever it is measured, even though the individual figures are not comparable.

## Evidence

| Indicator | Value | Reported by | What is measured |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New code generated by AI at Google | 75% (about 25% in October 2024, 50% in autumn 2025) | Sundar Pichai, April 2026 [S1] | New code "generated by AI and approved by engineers" |
| Merged lines written by Claude at Anthropic | Over 80% (a few percent in February 2025) | Anthropic, May 2026 [S3] | Self-reported by the vendor |
| Average share of AI-written code, 400+ companies | 51.9% (27.4% in Q1 2026, see objections) | DX, June 2026 [S3] | Instrumented measurement on repositories |
| Committed code generated or assisted by AI | 42%, with 65% expected in 2027 | Sonar, January 2026 [S2] | Survey of 1,100 developers |
| Stripe pull requests with no human-written line | Over 1,300 per week | Stripe, February 2026 [S3] | Absolute count; these PRs are reviewed by humans |

Source keys refer to [sources.md](../sources.md).

## Objections

- **These figures count lines**, the cheapest thing an agent produces. They say nothing about value delivered.
- **Definitions differ** (generated, assisted, approved, merged) and most numbers are self-reported.
- **The DX comparison point looks wrong.** Going from 27.4% in Q1 2026 to 51.9% in June 2026 would mean doubling in one quarter. The baseline is probably an earlier quarter. To verify.
- **Single secondary source.** The Anthropic, DX and Stripe figures all come from one compilation [S3], not from the original publications.
- "Approved by engineers" does not say how much attention each approval received. See [automation bias](../concepts/automation-bias.md).
