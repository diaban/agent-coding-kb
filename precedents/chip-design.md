# Chip design

## What was automated

Logic synthesis, placement and routing.

## What replaced human inspection

Simulation, formal equivalence checking, and automatic design rule checks.

## Lesson for code

When generation is automatic, verification becomes the core of the job and most of the effort.

## Where the analogy breaks

A chip has a formal specification to check against, and a manufacturing error is extremely expensive, which justified the investment in verification. Most software has no formal specification and can be patched after release.
